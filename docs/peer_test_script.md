## Before You Start

You'll create your own account using your BYU email. If you have Canvas connected at BYU, you can sync your real assignments. If not, you'll add a couple manually — the AI features work either way.

---

## Test 1 — Sign Up & Onboarding (~3 min)

1. Open **http://192.168.1.150:4173**
2. Click **Sign Up**, enter your BYU email and a password, click **Create Account**
3. You'll see the onboarding wizard:
   - **Welcome screen** → click Next
   - **Class source** — pick whichever applies (Canvas, Learning Suite, or Both). If you're not sure, pick **Canvas**
   - **Connect Canvas** — paste your Canvas personal access token (Settings → Account → Access Tokens in canvas.byu.edu) OR click **Skip for now**
   - **Learning Suite** — add your iCal feed URL if you chose LS, OR click **Skip**
   - **Survey questions** — answer honestly (study time, how involved you want the AI)
4. You should land on the **Dashboard**

**What to check:** Did onboarding feel clear? Was anything confusing?

---

## Test 2 — Add a Manual Assignment (~2 min)

If you skipped Canvas/LS, you need some assignments to test with.

1. Click the **+** button in the top-right of the dashboard header
2. Fill in:
   - Title: `STRAT 490R Final`
   - Course: `STRAT 490R`
   - Due date: next Thursday
3. Click **Add**
4. Add one more: `Econ 110 Midterm`, due this Friday
5. Both should appear in the assignment list on the left

**What to check:** Did the cards appear? Do they show the due date correctly?

---

## Test 3 — Weekly Schedule & Generate Plan (~3 min)

1. Click **Weekly** tab at the top
2. Click **Generate Plan** — the AI will build a study schedule for the next 7 days
3. Look at the calendar:
   - Do you see study blocks for your assignments?
   - Is Sunday empty? (intentional — no scheduling on Sundays)
   - Are exam/midterm assignments labeled "Study for …"?
   - Do past time slots look faded/grayed?
4. Try **dragging** a block to a different time slot
5. Try **resizing** a block by dragging the bottom edge

**What to check:** Does the schedule look reasonable? Is drag/resize smooth?

---

## Test 4 — AI Chat (~3 min)

1. Click the **chat bubble** button (bottom-right of the page)
2. Try these prompts one at a time:
   - *"What should I work on first today?"*
   - *"Help me plan my week — ask me some questions first"*
   - *"I'm overwhelmed, help me figure out what to cut"*
3. After the AI proposes a plan, click **Apply as my schedule**
4. Switch to the **Weekly** tab — the calendar should update

**What to check:** Did the AI ask clarifying questions before making a plan? Was the response useful? Did "Apply as my schedule" work?

---

## Test 5 — Assignment Detail (~2 min)

1. Click on any assignment card in the left panel
2. Set a time estimate (e.g., 90 minutes)
3. Change the status to **In Progress**
4. Add a note: *"Need to review chapters 3–5"*
5. Click **Save**

**What to check:** Did the changes save? Does the card reflect the updated status?

---

## Test 6 — Settings (~1 min)

1. Click the gear icon (top-right)
2. Change your **Involvement Level** to Proactive (or whatever you haven't tried)
3. Save preferences
4. Go back to Dashboard — the sidebar AI behavior should reflect the change
